PBR3D

Choose a procedural surface below, generated without downloads: building materials, pavement, tree bark, moss and soil. Metal-bearing presets use authored metallic maps; dielectric presets stay nonmetallic. The grid multiplies those factors: metalness increases left → right and roughness increases bottom → top. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Compare shadows, lights, environment, fog and HDR/bloom. Finishes add anisotropy, iridescence, subsurface wrap, parallax, wetness, snow, dirt, damage, detail and triplanar response on the same maps.
