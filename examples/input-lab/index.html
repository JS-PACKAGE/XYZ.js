Input Lab

Click the canvas, then move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to boost and press Space to fire. The same named actions also respond to a standard-mapping gamepad (D-pad or left stick, A to boost, X to fire) if one is connected. Move or click the pointer on the canvas to see its state.
