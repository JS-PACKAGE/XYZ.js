Physics2D lab

Drag any dynamic body with the mouse joint. The pendulum and chain hang from revolute and distance joints, the cup on the right is one static concave polygon, and the inner shelf is a chain collider. Fire the bullet at the thin wall with continuous collision on or off, drop balls and let them fall asleep, and toggle the debug overlay (static blue, awake green, sleeping gray, contacts red, joints cyan).
